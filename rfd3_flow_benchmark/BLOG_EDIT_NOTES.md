# Blog editing notes

The [working draft](BLOG_DRAFT.md) kept the experiment accurate, but it opened with a broad summary, introduced equations before the experimental question had much shape, and ended with generic next steps. Its repeated “not this, not that” phrasing also made the prose feel defensive.

What still sounded generated in the first rewrite: a few tidy transitions (“The translation was the interesting part,” “There are two limits”), a formulaic closing, and a figure caption that did not explain the scale of the null effect. I cut those transitions and made the completed 1CRN comparison the central plot and narrative hook.

The [final post](BLOG_POST.md) keeps the defensible claims: real checkpoint and parameter update, exact conditional densities for the modified sampler, four complete paired 1CRN seeds, a near-zero measured effect, and the incomplete held-out benchmark. It does not turn Cα geometry into a biological performance claim or imply that the held-out PDBs were absent from pretraining.
