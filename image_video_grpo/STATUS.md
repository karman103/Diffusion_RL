# Image/video GRPO study status

**Completed 2026-11-14 at 22:27 UTC.** This directory is separate from the earlier MNIST, Genie, RFdiffusion3, and PDB Flow-GRPO artifacts. No paid compute, publishing, or external contact was used.

- The pinned Google CIFAR-10 DDPM checkpoint (`267b167dc01f0e4e61923ea244e8b988f84deb80`) and 50-step stochastic DDIM policy passed finite-sample and exact zero-adapter log-ratio preflight on MPS. The frozen backbone has 35,746,307 parameters; the image residual adapter has 2,627. Checkpoint SHA-256 and all seeds are in `PROTOCOL.json`.
- The disjoint proxy and gold CIFAR-10 classifiers passed their predeclared held-out accuracy gates: 46.2% (required 40%) and 79.88% (required 60%). The CIFAR archive and both model files were rehashed after the study and matched the protocol.
- Three predeclared image training seeds (1101–1103) each completed 12 group-of-four updates. All 36 groups had nonzero advantages. Adapter delta norms were 3.61–3.69 × 10⁻⁵, maximum logged absolute action log-ratio was 0.000488, and no selected ratio was clipped. Training and evaluation ran sequentially after unrelated GPU jobs finished; no existing job was stopped or duplicated.
- All 384 predeclared image evaluation rows are complete (three adapters × 32 evaluation seeds × stock/zero/trained/best-of-four). The primary trained-minus-zero DDIM effect was +0.00000055 proxy frog probability, with a descriptive hierarchical 95% bootstrap interval [−0.0000117, +0.0000146]. Independent frog probability changed by −0.0000116 [−0.000107, +0.000046], and independent frog-class accuracy did not change on any pair. The measured result is **null at this budget and learning rate**. Stock DDPM and best-of-four are contextual baselines with different sampler or compute cost.
- `AUDIT.json` verifies every saved raw sample tensor's shape and finiteness, checks SHA-256, preserves evaluation-seed pairing, and recomputes all scores on CPU. Maximum absolute CPU-versus-MPS score discrepancy was 0.00000595. The sampled zero/trained images differed by 0.000088–0.000151 mean absolute pixel value across the three adapters. `RESULTS.md`, `paired_effects.png`, and `sample_grid.png` present the measured findings; `BLOG_POST.md` is the final employer-facing account.
- The toy-video checkpoint was pinned and loaded, but its four fixed four-frame feasibility samples had no detected digits and zero independent digit confidence. Two still earned motion reward. `VIDEO_GATE_FAILED.json` records the predeclared stop decision. **No video GRPO training or benchmark improvement is claimed.** The modified noisy Euler sampler is not the released deterministic sampler or the Flow-GRPO paper's marginal-equivalent construction.
- Seven numerical tests passed again after the run; source compilation passed. The image study has exact conditional Gaussian action densities for its modified sampler, not a final-image likelihood. Its clipped ratios are an optimization surrogate. The small-sample feature Fréchet score is not official FID.

The guarded queue finished with `QUEUE_DONE.json` and no failure marker. The task-scoped `caffeinate -i -t 21600` process (PID 56070) was stopped after completion, and the queue process exited; unrelated keep-awake processes were left alone. The dedicated `image-video-grpo-study` heartbeat was paused after the deliverables were finished.

A third, prospectively fixed [stronger-reward study](strong_reward/STATUS.md) produced a proxy reward gain but no reliable independent gain. A [native toy-video recheck](video_revisit/README.md) and corrected-sampler diagnostic both failed the digit-content gate; the pinned checkpoint's internal epoch is 4 despite its epoch-79 filename. See the [follow-up blog](FOLLOWUP_BLOG_POST.md). No video GRPO was run.

**Later local toy-video rescue (2026-11-15 UTC):** A separate [factorized video generator](video_factorized/STATUS.md) samples a canonical digit from the pretrained MNIST DDPM, then applies deterministic seeded translation across four frames. It passed a 6/8 prospective content gate and enabled four actual video-scored diffusion GRPO adapter runs. Neither the initial nor higher-step-size setting showed a reproducible trained-minus-zero quality gain; best-of-four proxy selection improved independent recognition at 4× sampling cost. This is not a successful learned-video diffusion model. The earlier “No video GRPO was run” statement describes the preceding study, not this later rescue. See the [audited results](video_factorized/RESULTS.md) and [video research blog](video_factorized/VIDEO_RESCUE_BLOG_POST.md).
