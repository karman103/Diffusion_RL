# Learned toy video diffusion + GRPO: results

**What was built.** A 1.7M-parameter factorised (2+1)D UNet video denoiser, trained from scratch
for 8,000 steps (~1h50m on an M3 laptop) on fresh Moving-MNIST clips (8 frames, 32x32, one digit
moving in one of 8 requested directions). The motion is generated by the network, not pasted.
GRPO then fine-tuned the whole denoiser on its own stochastic DDIM(eta=1, 40-step) transitions with
a checkable reward (requested direction + legible digit). Everything was fixed in
[PROTOCOL.json](PROTOCOL.json) before training. Changes made during the run are listed under
"Deviations".

## Gate (passed)
On 64 held-out samples from the pretrained model: 100% moved in the requested direction (median
error 0.55 deg by phase correlation), and 62.5% had a legible digit (gate: >= 60%). See `runs/gate_grid.png`.

## Main result (256 paired clips, identical noise per clip across policies)
Success = the evaluator CNN (trained on MNIST data the reward never saw) reads a legible, consistent
digit, AND the phase-correlation displacement is within 22.5 deg of the requested direction.

| Policy | Success | Δ vs pretrained (95% CI) | Digit-class entropy | Clips read as "8" | Δ success excluding 8s (post hoc) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pretrained | 52.0% | n/a | 0.92 | 64 | n/a |
| + CFG w=2 | 57.4% | +5.5 (−3.1, +13.7) | 0.88 | 57 | +4.2 (−6.5, +14.9) |
| Best-of-4 (4x compute) | 61.7% | +9.8 (+2.3, +17.2) | 0.95 | 57 | +11.9 (+3.0, +20.8) |
| GRPO seed 2101 | 62.5% | +10.5 (+2.7, +18.8) | 0.73 | 127 | +5.1 (−6.8, +16.9) |
| GRPO seed 2102 | 68.4% | +16.4 (+8.2, +24.6) | 0.65 | 150 | +2.1 (−11.7, +14.9) |
| GRPO seed 2103 | 60.2% | +8.2 (0.0, +16.4) | 0.58 | 109 | 0.0 (−12.9, +12.9) |
| **GRPO pooled** | 63.7% | **+11.7 (+4.8, +18.5)** | n/a | n/a | n/a |

CIs are clip-level bootstraps. With only three training seeds, they do not capture between-seed variance.

## Interpretation
1. **GRPO works mechanically on a learned video diffusion model.** All three seeds improved the
   independent success metric over the same noise, and the pooled interval excludes zero.
2. **Most of the gain is reward hacking by mode collapse.** Every seed drifted toward "8" (64 → 109–150
   of 256 clips), and digit-class entropy fell from 0.92 to 0.58–0.73. When clips read as 8 are
   excluded, the GRPO gain is 0–5 points with intervals spanning zero. Best-of-4 reaches a similar
   overall gain while keeping diversity, and its gain survives the exclusion.
3. **The motion constraint had no headroom.** The pretrained model already follows the requested
   direction 100% of the time, so all of GRPO's measured change is in legibility.
4. **Seed 2103 became unstable at the end.** Training reward was ~0.88 until update 146, then
   dropped to 0 over updates 147–150 because frames got too bright and failed the ink guard. The
   protocol evaluates final weights and declared no early stopping, so the post-collapse weights are
   what was evaluated. The independent evaluator ignores ink, which is why it still shows +8.2.
5. **The ink guard was loosely calibrated.** It was calibrated on real clips (mean ink 0.046), but
   the pretrained model's grey background puts 28% of its frames over the 0.15 limit. Part of the
   training-reward rise is GRPO darkening the background (seed 2102: corner level 0.040 → 0.001).
   This moves outputs toward real data, but it is a guard effect, not legibility.

**Bottom line:** this is a real, end-to-end video GRPO pipeline on a model that learned motion. Its
measured improvement mostly comes from collapsing onto an easy class, the same diversity-collapse
failure reported for Flow-GRPO and DanceGRPO at scale. Making the requested digit part of the
condition and the checkable reward is the obvious next fix, because collapsing onto one class would
then lower the reward.

## Deviations from the first protocol draft (all made before the affected stage ran)
- Canvas reduced from 48x48 to 32x32 and digits from 28 to 20 px, after measuring 3 s/step on MPS.
- The reward classifier failed its 95% gate twice (MLP 92.1%, global-pool CNN 80.0%). Those records
  are in `runs/classifiers_attempt*_failed.json`. The third architecture passed at 96.3%; the
  evaluator scored 98.3%.
- GRPO log-ratios and the KL are averaged over pixels, following the public Flow-GRPO code.
